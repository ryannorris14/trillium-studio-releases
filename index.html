Installing

Both computers will warn you once about an app from “an unidentified developer.” That’s expected — Trillium Studio is built by Ryan for the school, not sold in an app store. Here’s exactly what to click.

On Windows
  1. Download and open TrilliumStudio-Setup.exe.
  2. If a blue box says “Windows protected your PC”: click More info, then Run anyway. (The button is hidden until you click More info.)
  3. Click through the installer — it takes a few seconds and puts Trillium Studio on your desktop and Start menu.
  4. Open it. Your web browser opens with the app running — that’s normal; the whole thing lives on your computer.
First time in the app? Click Files ▾ → Open a .trillium file… (top right) and pick the file Ryan sent you. Courses, rooms, teachers, students — it’s all in that one file.
On a Mac
  1. Download and open the .dmg, then drag Trillium Studio into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. If the Mac says it “cannot verify the developer”, close that message, then go to → System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
  3. Open it again. Your web browser opens with the app running — that’s normal; the whole thing lives on your computer.
First time in the app? Click Files ▾ → Open a .trillium file… (top right) and pick the file Ryan sent you. Courses, rooms, teachers, students — it’s all in that one file.

Updating

When Ryan says there’s a new version: come back to this page, download the same button again, and install it over the old one. Your schedules and edits are kept — they live outside the app.

A note on student privacy: the installer contains no student information at all. Student data enters only through the .trillium file Ryan hands you directly, stays on your computer, and never touches the internet — the app works entirely offline.
